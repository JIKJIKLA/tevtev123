# Airshow kickoff 9.9.5

The first VS AI opening is a five-second countdown. The ball falls independently from 54.5 m through the fixed roof hatch, makes two diminishing bounces, then eases to its exact serving position with zero velocity/spin at GO. The drone, grippers and rotors have been removed. Hatch frames stay at 30.5 m; leaf clearance is tested across the complete ball crossing. Soccar and both Heatseeker serve positions retain their normal authority/solver state during presentation. Subsequent kickoffs, practice and multiplayer keep their existing countdowns. The original 1% AI bomb remains at first impact and does not award normal progress.

Three F-22s share the supplied model buffers and PBR textures. The leader flies straight at 110 m/s with an orange afterburner and smoke trail; blue-trail wingmen bank into opposite turns. Analytic paths keep all aircraft above the roof and maintain consistent trail curves after skipped frames. Six fixed 64-segment ribbons provide soft smoke and luminous exhaust without accumulating particles or allocating geometry per frame. No dynamic jet lights, shadow passes, scene captures or refraction are added. A wide, centered opening camera frames the split before easing back to the ordinary chase camera. Pass-driven shake stays under 12 cm, with HUD offsets capped at 3.5 × 2.5 pixels. The saved Airshow Camera & HUD Shake option and reduced motion disable it; pause and scene cleanup remove UI offsets.

Every playlist uses a static 1024 × 512 HDR stadium lighting probe: the approved photographic sky above, modeled concourse bands and blue/orange illuminated fascia around the horizon, and green turf bounce below. It is filtered once during loading by the existing PMREM environment pipeline and disposed after filtering. This is image-based lighting, **not hardware ray tracing**. Matte car paint remains unchanged. Non-color baked perimeter ambient occlusion grounds the turf in the standard preset; Detail retains the existing screen-space AO, bloom and sun shadows. Native-resolution policy and the approved sky image are unchanged.

Rendering references: [Three.js PMREMGenerator](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/PMREMGenerator.html), [MeshStandardMaterial](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/MeshStandardMaterial.html). Source retrieval was checked against the pinned Three.js 0.186.1 API. Sources describe the rendering techniques, not a claim that WebGL provides RTX.

Validation includes ball/door clearance, non-linear rest at zero, all team sizes and both playlists, reduced-motion exclusions, camera/UI bounds, symmetric split paths and continuous headings, fixed-size history-independent smoke, shared supplied mesh resources and colored exhaust, HDR linear-light conversion/orientation, and the existing client/server lifecycle suite. Browser screenshots and kickoff flow checks use the actual renderer. Asset provenance remains in [F22-ASSET.md](F22-ASSET.md).
