# Nivet League 10.1.1 — Refined audit record

Review date: 2026-10-28. This update reviews the game as a connected product: input and menus, local play, authoritative multiplayer, rendering, effects, sound, saved progress and release flow. It repairs concrete defects without changing the approved art direction, replacing recordings, copying new assets or adding paid resources.

This is a source and behavior review of the maintained game and its existing test coverage. It does not certify every byte of dependencies, every cosmetic variation, every browser/device or equality with Rocket League's proprietary engine. A static inspection, an automated regression and a live browser check are different forms of evidence; they are identified separately below.

## Shared gameplay and presentation boundaries

The review also follows the integration points that these fixes touch: Soccar and Heatseeker selection, 1v1/2v2/3v3 rosters, training versus online isolation, kickoff/countdown, goals/replay/results/rematch, camera and wall visibility, boost/pads, demolition/respawn, team paint, chat, saved bindings and progress. The existing matrix and physics regressions passed in the combined suites; merely reading other call sites is not an additional end-to-end pass. Live two-client and personal-paint checks cover their stated flows, not every possible network condition.

The 120 Hz independent simulation and Phoenix authority remain the gameplay architecture. Collision uses the game's documented compact approximation, rather than Rocket League's exact proprietary collision model. Client prediction and rendering feedback cannot award authoritative goals or online progress. Existing custom AI opening/airshow, visual effects, matte paint, photographic sky, black/velvet menu identity and blue/orange arena direction are retained.

No new images, audio recordings, generated assets, subscriptions or paid resources are introduced by this audit. Existing license/credit records remain applicable. Hosting remains subject to the account's actual plan and usage; this document does not promise universally free operation.

## Residual work and honest limits

- Bunny's unversioned root remains an observed stale cache; use the verified release link above. Existing open game tabs also retain their loaded JavaScript until refreshed.
- Phone garage scroll/focus and native browser selects have actual browser evidence. Continue hardware testing of a real gamepad through a completed match and mobile keyboard behavior; automated DOM events do not certify those platform-specific paths.
- Reconnect/session resumption and public backfill remain outside this update. Cancellation/forfeit and cleanup provide a defined outcome, not seamless recovery.
- Browser graphics/context availability, shader compilation time, network latency and device thermals vary. No Chromebook/mobile hardware performance certification or universal frame-rate claim is made.
- This is a comprehensive review of the maintained systems and identified defects, not a claim of exact 1:1 handling, every Rocket League effect/cosmetic or an exhaustive security audit of every dependency.
