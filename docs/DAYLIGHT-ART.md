# Daylight stadium sky

Asset: Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky)
Photograph: Greg Zaal. Sky edit: Jarod Guest, Poly Haven.
Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky

The downloaded tone-mapped panorama was reduced to a 2048 x 1024 JPEG (192,240 bytes) with bicubic resizing. No AI generation was used. Only the CC0 panorama is included, not Poly Haven's example renders or branding.

The portable HTML embeds this image. It is decoded during initial arena preload with a built-in fallback and needs no third-party requests during play. A 1024 x 512 copy, with a green lower hemisphere for pitch bounce, is filtered once for environment lighting. No live reflection captures, new dynamic lights, or mandatory postprocessing passes are added. The visible background is independent of physical illumination; exposure and fill light keep the pitch readable. The photographed source is tone-mapped LDR, not an HDR radiance lighting capture.

Direction: defined blue sky/clouds, warm-neutral sunlight, soft daylight fill, natural green turf and blue/orange stadium accents. Body paint retains zero clearcoat, low metalness and high roughness with broad highlights.
